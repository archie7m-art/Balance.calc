Business Calculator

Balance sheet, profit and GST, sales and stock. Open a file or just type numbers, press a button. Nothing leaves your phone or computer.

1. Get your data in (the quick calculators need no file)

Works with CSV, Excel (.xlsx), Word (.docx), PDF that has real text, or text you paste. You can also drop a file here.

2. Your table (tap any cell to change it)

Each column is marked Names (text), Numbers (amounts) or Other. Each row has a Type (asset, liability, equity, total…). I guess both from the words; change them if I guessed wrong.

3. Pick a section, then a button (53 tools)

Pick a tool above

The rules (what counts as an asset, a total, a balance; which column is the amount) are built in by the author and guessed from the words in your table. Always look at the table before you trust an answer. "This would make it balance" is arithmetic, not proof of the real mistake. GST rates and rules change: confirm with your accountant. Rules of thumb (good ratio, margin) depend on the kind of business.
