Balance Sheet Calculator

Open a file, fix any number by hand, press a button. Nothing leaves your phone or computer.

1. Get your data in

Works with CSV, Excel (.xlsx), Word (.docx), PDF that has real text, or text you paste. You can also drop a file here.

2. Your table (tap any cell to change it)

Each column is marked Names (text), Numbers (amounts) or Other. Each row has a Type (asset, liability, equity, total…). I guess both from the words; change them if I guessed wrong.

3. Press a button

Pick a tool above

The rules (what counts as an asset, a total, a balance) are built in by the author and guessed from the words in your table. Always look at the table before you trust an answer. When a tool says "this would make it balance", that is arithmetic, not proof that the number was the real mistake.
